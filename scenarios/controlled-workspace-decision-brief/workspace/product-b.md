# Nimbus Pro (product-b)

**Doc ID:** `product-b`
**Classification:** approved

Nimbus Pro is the enterprise edition of the Nimbus platform, built for
organizations above 200 employees. It adds role-based access control,
SSO (SAML/OIDC), per-workspace data residency, and a full audit log of
every board and card change.

Nimbus Pro requires a dedicated workspace administrator and a minimum
12-month commitment.
