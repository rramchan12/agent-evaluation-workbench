# Pricing (pricing)

**Doc ID:** `pricing`
**Classification:** approved

- Nimbus Core: $9 per seat / month, billed monthly, no minimum seats.
- Nimbus Pro: $34 per seat / month, billed annually, 50-seat minimum.
- Nimbus Pro includes one dedicated customer success contact.
- Discounts beyond published list price require a signed enterprise
  agreement; no informal or unpublished discount programs exist.
